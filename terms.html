Clip Factory is an internal video processing and publishing tool used by its operator. It is not offered as a public SaaS or consumer service.

YouTube terms

By using this API Client, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Use of YouTube functionality is subject to those terms and applicable Google/YouTube API policies, including the YouTube API Services Terms of Service and YouTube Developer Policies. Google's data practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Operator responsibilities

The operator is responsible for selecting content they are authorized to process and upload, including obtaining the necessary rights and permissions. Clip Factory does not claim ownership of third-party content merely because it processes or uploads it.

The operator is responsible for uploaded metadata and visibility settings. The factory can publish publicly according to the operator's configured authorization and schedule, subject to YouTube's restrictions. A separate manual upload tool also offers private, unlisted and public visibility choices.

The tool sets Made for Kids to No. If content is made for kids, the operator must immediately go to YouTube on desktop and declare it as made for kids.

Authorization and disconnecting

Google handles sign-in; the tool stores OAuth authorization locally and does not collect or store YouTube/Google passwords. The in-tool Revoke YouTube Access feature programmatically revokes Google's authorization after confirmation and deletes locally stored OAuth authorization after successful revocation. Google Security Permissions is an additional revocation method. See the Privacy Policy's data deletion section for local cleanup details.

Disconnecting does not delete videos already stored on YouTube. Manage those separately in YouTube Studio.

Nothing on this site implies endorsement or partnership with YouTube or Google. References to those services describe the API integration only.
